Are you a ride-share driver wondering if a trip is worth taking?

TripWorth reads the offer on your screen and shows an instant verdict:

• Accept / Caution / Skip — clear decision in seconds
• RON/km and RON/hour — including net profit after fuel
• Pickup distance, estimated duration, customizable thresholds
• Overlay on top of other apps — see the verdict without switching screens
• Shift history and statistics

100% local on your phone. No account. No data sent to servers.

You set the rules — TripWorth only calculates.

Compatible with your driver apps. Works offline after setup.
