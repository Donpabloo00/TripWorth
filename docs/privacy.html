TripWorth — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

TripWorth helps ride-share drivers decide whether a trip is worth accepting. Your privacy matters: all data is processed locally on your device.

What we use

  • On-screen content (OCR) to read ride offers
  • Your settings (minimum fare, fuel cost, custom rules)
  • Local history of analyzed offers on your device

What we do NOT collect

  • No account or sign-in required
  • No data sent to external servers
  • No sale of personal data
  • No advertising trackers

Android permissions

  • Screen capture — to analyze offers shown in other apps
  • Display over other apps — for the verdict overlay
  • Notifications — for the background reading service
  • Battery optimization exemption — optional, so the service is not stopped mid-shift

Storage and deletion

All data stays on your device. You can clear cache and history in the app. Uninstalling removes all local data.

Contact

For privacy questions: support@tripworth.app
